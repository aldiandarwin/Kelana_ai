# Packing Checklist Brief

> Sinaptik Travel internal knowledge base. Course fixture for KelanaAI Session 9.
> Revision 2026-08-01. The allowance figures below are Sinaptik Travel policy.
> The airline operating the flight may apply a lower limit.

## The rule that decides everything else

Pack to the tighter of two numbers, the airline allowance and the Sinaptik Travel
allowance. They are not the same, and the airline wins at the check-in desk.
Sinaptik Travel's allowance governs what the operator will carry on transfers,
porterage, and internal legs.

Full baggage figures live in `sinaptik-travel-policy.md`. This brief covers what
goes inside the bag.

## Documents, carried on the person

- Passport, valid six months beyond the return date
- Visa or entry registration printout, even when an electronic record exists
- Printed flight itinerary
- Insurance certificate with the twenty-four hour assistance number
- Two photocopies of the passport photo page, stored separately from the passport

Sinaptik Travel asks travellers to photograph every document and store the images
in an offline folder on the phone. A cloud folder is useless without a connection.

## Electronics

Japan, South Korea, and Singapore all use different plug shapes. Japan uses the
flat two-pin Type A at 100 volts. South Korea uses the round two-pin Type C and F
at 220 volts. Singapore uses the rectangular three-pin Type G at 230 volts.

A universal adapter covers all three. A voltage converter is only needed for
appliances that are not dual-voltage, which today means hair dryers and little
else.

Power banks must travel in the cabin, never in checked baggage. Most carriers cap
a single power bank at 100 watt-hours without prior approval.

## Clothing by season

For a March or April trip to Tokyo or Seoul, pack layers. Daytime is mild and
evenings drop close to freezing.

For June through August anywhere in the region, pack for humidity rather than
heat. Quick-drying fabric, a compact umbrella, and a second pair of shoes.

For November through February in Seoul, pack a genuine winter coat. Seoul in
January is materially colder than Tokyo in January, and travellers routinely
under-pack for it.

## Temples, shrines, and religious sites

Shoulders and knees covered. Slip-on shoes save time at sites that require
removing footwear, which in Japan is most temple interiors.

## What Sinaptik Travel will not carry

The operator's transfer vehicles will not accept surfboards, bicycles, or any
single item over thirty-two kilograms. These require a freight booking arranged
at least fourteen days ahead and quoted separately.
