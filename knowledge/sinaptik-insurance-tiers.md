# Sinaptik Travel Insurance Tiers and Limits

> Sinaptik Travel internal knowledge base. Course fixture for KelanaAI Session 9.
> Added in the Session 9 homework expansion. Revision 2026-08-01.
> Every figure below is an invented Sinaptik Travel product term. Sinaptik Travel
> is a fictional operator. These are not market rates and not a real contract.
> The vocabulary used here is explained in `travel-insurance-basics.md`.

## Three tiers, one mandatory choice

Every Sinaptik Travel booking must carry one of three insurance tiers. A
traveller who declines cover signs a waiver and is refused transfer service on
medical grounds.

| Tier | Product code | Premium per traveller per day |
|---|---|---|
| Essential | SNP-INS-ESS | IDR 42,000 |
| Voyager | SNP-INS-VOY | IDR 78,000 |
| Summit | SNP-INS-SUM | IDR 145,000 |

Premiums are charged per calendar day of the itinerary, counted from the
departure date to the return date inclusive. A seven-day trip is billed as seven
days even when the flights land at midnight.

## Emergency medical evacuation limits

This is the figure travellers ask about most and the one that differs most
between tiers.

| Tier | Emergency medical evacuation limit | Repatriation of remains |
|---|---|---|
| Essential | IDR 250,000,000 | IDR 100,000,000 |
| Voyager | IDR 750,000,000 | IDR 250,000,000 |
| Summit | IDR 2,000,000,000 | IDR 500,000,000 |

Evacuation must be authorised in advance by the Sinaptik assistance desk on
+62 21 5000 7788. A traveller who arranges their own air ambulance without
authorisation is reimbursed at the Essential limit of IDR 250,000,000 regardless
of which tier was purchased.

## Overall medical expense limits

| Tier | Overall medical limit | Deductible per claim |
|---|---|---|
| Essential | IDR 150,000,000 | IDR 1,500,000 |
| Voyager | IDR 500,000,000 | IDR 750,000 |
| Summit | IDR 1,500,000,000 | Nil |

Outpatient dental is covered for emergency pain relief only and is capped at
IDR 5,000,000 on all three tiers.

## Baggage and delay benefits

| Tier | Baggage loss | Baggage delay past 6 hours | Trip delay past 6 hours |
|---|---|---|---|
| Essential | IDR 7,500,000 | IDR 1,000,000 | IDR 750,000 |
| Voyager | IDR 20,000,000 | IDR 2,500,000 | IDR 1,500,000 |
| Summit | IDR 50,000,000 | IDR 5,000,000 | IDR 3,000,000 |

A single article is capped at twenty-five per cent of that tier's baggage loss
limit. Electronics carry a further sub-limit of IDR 10,000,000 on Voyager and
Summit, and are excluded entirely on Essential.

## Trip cancellation benefit

| Tier | Cancellation limit | Covered reasons |
|---|---|---|
| Essential | Not covered | None |
| Voyager | IDR 25,000,000 | Illness, injury, immediate family bereavement |
| Summit | IDR 60,000,000 | The Voyager reasons plus jury duty, redundancy, and visa refusal |

Visa refusal is covered on Summit only, and only when Sinaptik Travel filed the
application. A self-filed application that is refused is not a covered reason on
any tier.

## The Summit adventure rider

Sinaptik Travel classifies scuba diving below eighteen metres, via ferrata,
mountaineering above 4,000 metres, and any motorised watersport as extreme
activities. All three tiers exclude them by default.

The adventure rider, product code SNP-INS-ADV, attaches to Summit only. It costs
an additional IDR 95,000 per traveller per day and lifts the exclusion. It cannot
be attached to Essential or Voyager at any price.

## Exclusions that apply to every tier

- Any condition treated in the ninety days before departure, unless declared and
  accepted in writing before the policy is issued
- Travel undertaken against a published government advisory for that destination
- Loss of cash above IDR 2,000,000
- Claims filed more than thirty days after the incident
- Costs incurred after the traveller declined a repatriation offered by the
  assistance desk

## Upgrading mid-trip

A tier can be upgraded only before departure. Once the first flight has departed,
the tier is locked for the whole itinerary. This is the single most common
request the assistance desk has to refuse.
