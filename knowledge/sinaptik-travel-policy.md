# Sinaptik Travel Booking and Baggage Policy

> Sinaptik Travel internal knowledge base. Course fixture for KelanaAI Session 9.

## Baggage allowance

Sinaptik Travel's allowance is what the operator carries on transfers, porterage,
and internal legs. The airline operating a flight may apply a lower limit, and at
the check-in desk the airline always wins.

| Route type | Checked baggage per traveller | Cabin baggage per traveller |
|---|---|---|
| Domestic Indonesia | 20 kg | 7 kg |
| Regional Asia | 30 kg | 7 kg |
| International long haul | 40 kg | 10 kg |

Regional Asia covers Japan, South Korea, Singapore, Malaysia, Thailand, Vietnam,
and the Philippines. Everything beyond those seven is priced as international
long haul.

A single piece may not exceed 32 kilograms on any route type. That is a handling
limit, not a weight allowance, and it applies even when the traveller has weight
remaining.

Cabin baggage may not exceed 56 by 36 by 23 centimetres on any route.

## Excess baggage

Excess is charged per kilogram, per leg, and is payable at the time of booking.
Excess purchased at the airport is charged at the airline's own counter rate,
which Sinaptik Travel does not control and does not refund.

| Route type | Excess rate per kilogram |
|---|---|
| Domestic Indonesia | IDR 45,000 |
| Regional Asia | IDR 110,000 |
| International long haul | IDR 185,000 |

Excess is capped at 15 kilograms per traveller per leg. Beyond that the shipment
must be booked as freight, which requires fourteen days' notice.

## Sports and oversized items

Surfboards, bicycles, and golf sets travel as freight, never as baggage. Freight
is quoted per item and must be arranged at least fourteen days before departure.
The operator's transfer vehicles do not accept them.

## Cancellation windows and fees

Cancellation fees are calculated on the total package price and are counted in
calendar days before the departure date, not working days.

| Days before departure | Cancellation fee |
|---|---|
| 45 days or more | 10 per cent of package price |
| 44 to 30 days | 25 per cent of package price |
| 29 to 15 days | 50 per cent of package price |
| 14 to 8 days | 75 per cent of package price |
| 7 days or fewer | 100 per cent, no refund |

Visa fees, insurance premiums, and issued air tickets are never refundable at any
point in the schedule. They are deducted before the percentage is applied.

A cancellation is effective on the date Sinaptik Travel receives written notice
by email at booking@sinaptik.example. A phone call does not stop the clock.

## Date changes

One date change is permitted free of charge when requested 45 days or more before
departure. Every later change costs IDR 750,000 per traveller plus any fare
difference charged by the airline.

A date change requested inside 14 days is treated as a cancellation and rebooking,
at the cancellation fee for that window.

## Payment schedule

| Stage | Amount | Due |
|---|---|---|
| Booking deposit | 30 per cent of package price | At confirmation |
| Second instalment | 40 per cent of package price | 45 days before departure |
| Final balance | 30 per cent of package price | 21 days before departure |

A booking with an unpaid balance 21 days before departure is released
automatically, and the deposit is forfeited under the 29 to 15 day band.

## Name changes

A name correction of up to three characters is free. A full name change is a
cancellation and rebooking, because airlines treat the ticket as non-transferable.

## Group bookings

Ten travellers or more qualify as a group. Groups receive one free place per
twenty paying travellers, and the cancellation schedule shifts each band fifteen
days earlier. Group deposits are non-refundable in all circumstances.

## Complaints

Complaints must reach Sinaptik Travel within thirty days of the return date. The
operator responds within fourteen working days. Complaints raised after thirty
days are recorded but not compensated.
