# Singapore Entry and Operations Brief

> Sinaptik Travel internal knowledge base. Course fixture for KelanaAI Session 9.
> Added in the Session 9 homework expansion. Revision 2026-08-01.
> Entry rules are set by the Singapore government and change. Sinaptik Travel
> re-checks them before every departure. Operational figures are the operator's
> own policy, invented for this bootcamp.

## Who needs a visa

Singapore publishes an assessment-level list. Nationals on the visa-required list
must obtain a visa before travel. Everyone else is assessed at the checkpoint and
granted a period of stay by the immigration officer, not by the airline and not
by the operator.

Sinaptik Travel's standing instruction is that the requirement and the permitted
length of stay for any nationality must be confirmed on the ICA website before
every departure. Both have changed, and the operator does not answer from memory.

The critical point the operator repeats to every traveller is that a visa-free
arrangement grants eligibility to seek entry. It does not grant entry. The stamp
in the passport on arrival is what sets the actual permitted stay, and it can be
shorter than the maximum.

## SG Arrival Card

Every traveller must submit the SG Arrival Card electronically. The submission
window opens three days before arrival and there is no fee.

Sinaptik Travel submits it on behalf of travellers who booked a full package, and
sends a confirmation email once accepted. Travellers on transfer-only bookings
submit their own. The operator sends a reminder ninety-six hours before arrival
either way.

Only official channels are used. Third-party sites that charge for this
submission are a known scam and the operator warns travellers about them at
booking.

## What immigration asks for

- Passport valid at least six months beyond the arrival date
- Confirmed onward or return ticket
- Accommodation address for every night
- Evidence of sufficient funds

Sinaptik Travel supplies a printed itinerary and hotel confirmation to every
traveller because officers ask for the address and a phone screen in flight mode
is a slow way to answer.

## Prohibited and controlled items

Singapore's customs list is stricter than most in the region and the penalties are
real. Chewing gum for sale, e-cigarettes and vaporisers, shisha, and chewing
tobacco are all prohibited outright.

E-cigarettes are the single most common problem the operator sees. Possession is
an offence, not merely importation, so leaving the device at home is the only
safe course.

Duty-free allowances do not cover cigarettes. Every cigarette brought in is
dutiable, and there is no personal allowance at all.

## Money and transport

The currency is the Singapore dollar. Card and contactless acceptance is close to
universal, including on public transport, where a contactless bank card taps
directly at the gate.

Sinaptik Travel does not issue a separate transit card for Singapore. Travellers
tap their own contactless card, which the operator considers cheaper than the
tourist pass for itineraries under four days.

## Weather and scheduling

Singapore is hot and humid year round with no dry season worth planning around.
Afternoon thunderstorms are routine and brief.

Sinaptik Travel schedules outdoor segments before noon and keeps an indoor
alternative on every afternoon slot. The operator does not quote a seasonal
surcharge for Singapore because the weather does not move prices.

## Connectivity

A data eSIM is included on every Singapore package with a 3 GB allowance for
itineraries up to four days. Free public wifi is available at the airport, on
public transport, and across most malls.
