# Japan Entry Requirements Brief

> Sinaptik Travel internal knowledge base. Course fixture for KelanaAI Session 9.
> Revision 2026-08-01. Operational figures are Sinaptik Travel policy, not
> government policy. Travellers must confirm current rules with the embassy.

## Who needs a tourist visa

Japan operates a visa waiver arrangement with a list of countries. Travellers
from countries outside that list must obtain a short-stay tourist visa before
departure. The waiver, where it applies, covers tourism and short business
visits only. It never covers paid work.

Indonesian passport holders fall into two groups. Holders of an ordinary
passport must apply for a tourist visa. Holders of an e-passport may instead
register a visa exemption at a Japanese representative office, which is a
registration step rather than a visa application.

Sinaptik Travel treats both groups the same way operationally. Documents are
collected fourteen working days before departure regardless of which route the
traveller uses.

## Documents Sinaptik Travel collects

Every Japan booking requires six items before the file is considered complete.

- Passport valid for at least six months beyond the return date
- Two blank facing pages in the passport
- Completed application form supplied by Sinaptik Travel
- One photograph, 4.5 by 4.5 centimetres, white background, taken within six months
- Bank statement covering the last three months
- Return flight itinerary and confirmed accommodation for every night

Sinaptik Travel does not accept a bank statement showing a balance below
IDR 25,000,000 for a seven-day itinerary. That threshold is the operator's own
underwriting rule and is stricter than the consulate's guidance.

## Processing time

The consulate publishes a working guideline of four to seven working days once a
file is accepted. Sinaptik Travel adds its own buffer and quotes travellers ten
working days.

Files submitted during Golden Week, Obon, or the New Year period are quoted
fifteen working days. Sinaptik Travel does not accept a Japan booking with a
departure date closer than twenty calendar days.

## Medication rules

Japan restricts several substances that are sold over the counter elsewhere.
Pseudoephedrine and codeine are the two that most often catch travellers.

Travellers carrying prescription medication should bring the prescription and a
letter from the prescribing doctor, both in English. For quantities beyond one
month of personal use, an import certificate known as Yakkan Shoumei must be
obtained before departure.

Sinaptik Travel asks about medication at the time of booking, not at the airport.
The question is on the intake form because a traveller stopped at customs is a
problem the operator cannot solve remotely.

## What the tourist visa does not permit

A tourist visa does not permit paid work, enrolment in a course longer than
ninety days, or a change of status while inside Japan. Overstaying carries a
re-entry ban that Sinaptik Travel cannot appeal on the traveller's behalf.
